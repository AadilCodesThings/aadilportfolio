About

I’m Aadil, a designer, educator, and content creator exploring how we keep people at the center of design as technology changes around us.

I’ve spent 10 years doing research and design at IBM, BMO, and XE, and I teach UX in Toronto Metropolitan University’s Master of Digital Media program. But the work I care most about happens outside the job: making things that help people learn, create, and find their place in design.

I share design ideas with more than 6,000 people across Instagram and LinkedIn, and I’m one of ADPList’s Top 100 Mentors, with more than 10,000 minutes spent mentoring designers worldwide.
